## Part 2 — shape optimization

New crate `aero-optim` (depends on `aero-geometry` and `aero-foil2d`, not on
Tauri):

- design variables: CST coefficients of both surfaces (`aero_geometry::cst`),
  started from a least-squares fit of the initial airfoil; trailing-edge
  thickness kept;
- objectives: minimum drag at prescribed lift(s), maximum lift-to-drag
  ratio at prescribed lift(s) or angle(s), with weights for several
  operating points (polar optimization);
- constraints: minimum thickness (overall and at chord stations), minimum
  pitching moment, maximum thickness, trailing-edge angle, plus the
  requirement that every viscous point converges; handled by penalties;
- optimizers: CMA-ES (global, population evaluated in parallel on all
  cores, seeded and reproducible) followed by Nelder–Mead (local polish);
- progress callback with cancellation, history of evaluations, best
  airfoil exported as a regular `Airfoil`.

- **P2a** — crate, design space, objective/constraint evaluation, tests
  (CST round trip, penalties, failure handling).
- **P2b** — optimizers, tested on analytic problems (sphere, Rosenbrock,
  constrained problems) and for determinism.
- **P2c** — end-to-end tests: drag reduction at prescribed lift for a NACA
  section with the thickness constraint satisfied; verification of the
  optimized airfoil by an independent analysis.
- **P2d** — application: "Optimize" tab (problem definition, live progress,
  shape and polar comparison, cancel, add the result to the library),
  project file, guided tour.
- **P2e** — documentation and benchmarks.
